# PLAN – Trade network, village connections, house fine-tuning (Villagers: Repurposed 0.9.0)

Status: 2026-10-29. Fabric mod, MC 26.2, Java 25, package `de.villagerplus`. Code: `src/main/java/de/villagerplus/**`. Test: `runServer`/`runClient` with RCON (see `AI-HANDOFF.md`).

## Goal / acceptance criteria
1. **Trader arrives visibly.** A caravan (wandering trader + llamas) appears 70–100 blocks from the bell (only if chunks are loaded there, otherwise fallback to the previous appearance near the bell) and **walks visibly to the village chest**, stays there for a while and walks away again. The player can **talk to the trader normally and trade with them** (vanilla trade menu), independently of the automatic village exchange.
2. **Imaginary partner villages.** Every village has 3 deterministic, fictional partner places (name from the seed of the bell position, direction, specialty: e.g. ore, fabrics, food, wood). Each caravan is assigned a partner (trader name “Händler aus <Ort>” (trader from <place>)), its offer reflects the specialty. **Relationship** per partner (−100..+100): rises through successful exchange, gifts, trade by the player; falls when there has been no visit for a long time. Relationship influences prices (up to ±25 %), visit frequency and the quality of the offer. Visible in the Dorftafel (village board).
3. **Connecting villages.** At the Dorftafel (tab “Nachbarn” (neighbors)) the player can take a **Dorfkarte** (village map; new item `villagerplus:dorfkarte`, carries dimension (`ResourceKey` as string), bell position and name of the home village as `custom_data`). **Right-clicking the Dorftafel of another village with the map** connects both villages permanently (mutual entry, only if distance ≤ 3000 blocks, **dimension of the map = dimension of the board** and the source bell in that dimension is loaded and still exists (otherwise a message), not the same village; the map is consumed, both get a chat message).
4. **Connection menu.** In the tab “Nachbarn” each connected village shows: name, distance, direction, residents, relationship. Actions (server checks distance to the board ≤ 24, cooldown per action/pair):
   - **Waren tauschen** (exchange goods): for each village the respective largest surpluses (balance categories from `Balance`) are exchanged 1:1 by value, max. 64 items per direction and 12 000 ticks cooldown; **advance capacity check on both sides** (free slots in village chest + storage chests via a `Lager.freeSlot`-like count, without `Lager.deposit`, which throws `ItemEntity`s on the ground on overflow); only when both sides can safely take the incoming stacks is anything removed and inserted, otherwise rejection “Kein Platz im Zieldorf” (no room in the target village). Chests of both villages are **changed simultaneously**, therefore only if both chunks are loaded, otherwise the message “Das Nachbardorf ist nicht erreichbar (Chunks nicht geladen)” (the neighboring village is not reachable (chunks not loaded)).
   - **Handelsroute** (trade route, on/off): while active, the caravans of both villages preferably visit each other (visit interval ×0,6) and the relationship to partners falls more slowly.
   - **Gäste einladen** (invite guests): spawns up to 2 guests (`Guests`) in the neighboring village from one's own village (cost: 1 emerald from the village chest per guest) – if the neighboring village is loaded, otherwise a message.
   - **Gruß senden** (send greeting): +5 mutual relationship (cooldown 24 000 ticks), slightly raises `Wellbeing` mood on both sides (village mood bonus as a simple counter, not tied to individual villagers).
5. **House fine-tuning.** Less terracotta area: in terracotta villages (Overhauled Villages) only the **upper story/gable** is built from terracotta, the **lower story** from the stone/plaster of the neighboring houses (`Palette.layered` reads “below” and “above” separately; applies only if the neighboring houses show the same), plus **stone-brick corner pillars, a belt course** between the stories (stairs/slabs of plinth stone, stone window frames under the windows). Target in the image: terracotta ≤ 50 % of the wall area, visible stone details. Biome fit: the style comes from the **nearest 8 houses** (already implemented, `Palette.detectAt`); `Palette.detect(…, seed, site)` calls `curate` **only** if fewer than 3 beds were found for the measurement (the count is returned by `detectAt`); with ≥ 3 houses the measured palette remains unchanged (today `curate` is always called for vanilla walls – this will be changed).

## Design
- **Village identity is dimension-qualified**: `VillageId{dimension, bellPos}`; `Villages.villageNear`/`KNOWN_BELLS` are changed to `Map<ResourceKey<Level>, Set<Long>>` (today only `Set<Long>`), links store `dimension` + `bellPos` and are only resolved in the same `ServerLevel`.
- **New attachment** `VillagerPlus.NETWORK` (persistent, on the bell): `TradeNetwork{ List<Partner> partners; List<Link> links (`Link{dimension, bellPos, name, relation (−100..100, start 10), routeActive}`; greeting/actions update `relation` **in both opposite-direction links**, display in the tab “Nachbarn” reads it there); Map<String,Long> cooldowns; int moodBonus; long moodBonusUntil }` (greeting mood is persistent, default 0, expires after 24 000 ticks). `VillageData.Extra` already has the codec maximum of 16 fields, therefore no field there. Codec with `optionalFieldOf` and defaults (empty lists) for old worlds. Partners are generated deterministically from `bell.asLong()` on first access (no persisting needed except relationship/last visit).
- **Caravan** (`village/Caravan.java`): spawn location instead of “near bell”: `Sites.candidate(level, bell, rnd, 70, 100)` with ground check and loaded chunk (up to 20 attempts), otherwise old spawn. The trader gets `setCustomName("Händler aus X")` (trader from X), navigation via `getNavigation().moveTo(chest, 0.5)` until ≤ 4 blocks from the village chest, stay as before (`ACTIVE` state remains, `leaveAt`), way back to the spawn point. The existing automatic exchange (`exchange`) remains. Player trade: the vanilla offers are replaced at spawn by `partnerOffers(partner, relation)` (sell the specialty cheaply, buy village surpluses); relationship gain **exclusively via the offer usage delta** (see review dispositions round 2/R8): every 4 ticks compare the sum of `MerchantOffer.getUses()`; delta > 0 = player trade, credit to the nearest player within a 12-block radius; `getTradingPlayer()` is not used for this.
- **Dorfkarte item** `ModItems.DORFKARTE`, registration like `ModBlocks` (`ResourceKey`, `setId`), item definition `assets/villagerplus/items/dorfkarte.json` with existing map texture variant (new 16×16 PNG), lang entries, recipe (paper + bell? → no: only obtainable via menu, no recipe). Linking via `UseBlockCallback` (server): hand item is a Dorfkarte, block is `DorftafelBlock`, `Villages.villageNear` yields target ≠ source.
- **Network**: new payloads `NetworkAction(BlockPos board, String action, UUID? , String arg)` (client→server) and extension of `BoardData` with `List<String> neighbors` (format `name|dist|richtung|einwohner|beziehung|route`), `partners` (`name|spez|beziehung|letzteBesuchTage`). The packet contains **no target coordinates and no village ID**: the server derives the source village from the verified board and only accepts an **index into the persisted link list** of the source village; each handler then checks dimension, loaded chunks of both bells, distance of the player to the board (≤ 24) and action-specific prerequisites. `BoardScreen` gets the tab “Nachbarn” (connected villages + 3 partner places, action buttons, “Dorfkarte mitnehmen” (take Dorfkarte)).
- **Stone details**: in `Architect` new elements only if `Palette.base` ≠ wall block: corner pillars (instead of half-timbering logs) from `base`, belt course at `wallH ≥ 4`, window sill from `roofSlab`/`base` slab. `Palette.layered` returns `wall` (below) and `wall2` (above); for terracotta villages: `wall` = neighbors-below material, never terracotta if the neighbors show something else below.

## Review dispositions round 5 (round limit reached, last version not re-reviewed)
R16 accepted: `Architect.build` limits terracotta afterwards deterministically to ≤ 50 % of the wall/gable blocks (lowest terracotta rows become plinth/stone material until the ratio fits); upper material starts at wall height/2. R17 accepted: stone details (window sills, belt course, corner pillars when the frame is missing) are activated by “terracotta involved or `wall2` ≠ `wall`”, not by `base` ≠ `wall`. R18 accepted: the `Villages` link checks before consuming the map whether the pair is already linked (idempotent: message “bereits verbunden” (already connected), map is kept).

## Review dispositions round 4
R12 accepted (caravan paragraph adjusted). R13 accepted: maximum of **12 links per village** (link attempt beyond that is rejected, message), so `neighbors` safely fits into `list(200)`. R14 accepted: `Link` additionally stores `population` and `populationDay`, refreshed when the neighboring village is loaded; otherwise the UI shows “Einwohner: zuletzt N (vor X Tagen)” (residents: last N (X days ago)). R15 accepted: beds are grouped into **buildings** before each sample (beds ≤ 6 blocks apart = one house, sample location = first bed), then the nearest 8 (palette) or 12 (profile) **different** houses; the same function `Palette.houses(level, center, limit)` for `detectAt`, `layered` and `profileCode`.

## Review dispositions round 3
R9 accepted: `Link.relation`/`routeActive` persistent per linked village, updated reciprocally. R10 accepted: `Caravan.State` stores `dimension` (`ResourceKey<Level>`); `ACTIVE` entries (including llama UUIDs) are only processed and deleted in their own `ServerLevel`. R11 accepted: verification 70–100 spawn / ≤ 4 arrival.

## Review dispositions round 2
R6 accepted: The caravan **explicitly spawns 2 `TraderLlama`** next to the trader (`EntityType.TRADER_LLAMA`, `EntitySpawnReason.EVENT`), leashed via `setLeashedTo(trader, true)`, carried in `ACTIVE.State` as a UUID list and removed (`discard`) when the trader leaves/disappears, cleanup also on load (orphaned llamas with tag `vp_caravan` without a trader are removed).
R7 accepted: **canonical pair key** `min(bellA,bellB)|max(bellA,bellB)|aktion`; every successful action writes the cooldown **atomically into both** `TradeNetwork` attachments; both directions check the same key.
R8 accepted: relationship gain via **offer usage delta**: `Caravan.tick` runs every 4 ticks, sums `MerchantOffer.getUses()` of all offers of the trader and compares with the last value; every delta > 0 counts as a completed player trade (player = nearest player within a 12-block radius, relationship +2 per delta, max. +10 per visit), independent of `getTradingPlayer()`.

## Review dispositions round 1
R1–R5 (Codex, all medium/low) accepted and incorporated above: R1 dimension in map/identity, R2 `moodBonus` in the attachment, R3 index instead of client target, R4 advance capacity check instead of `Lager.deposit`, R5 `curate` only with < 3 beds.

## Non-goals
No real pathfinding across multiple villages; no transport between villages in unloaded chunks (actions refuse); no new villager professions; no change to the trade menu's appearance; trader llamas remain vanilla.

## Risks
- Chunk not loaded → fallback to old behavior; navigation fails → trader is removed after a time via `leaveAt` (existing).
- Trader despawns in vanilla after `despawnDelay` (30 000 set) → ok.
- Linking two villages mutually: data consistency on unloading → linking only if both bell chunks are loaded.
- Item registration/components in 26.2: `custom_data` via `DataComponents.CUSTOM_DATA`.
