# Binding Functional Scope of the User Master Prompt

Source: the user's complete assignment in this chat dated 29.10.2026. This is a verifiable outline of its requirements. Concept images `image_2`, `image_3`, `image_4` are not available here as image files; their concrete pixel contents are not invented. Latest user corrections: continuous automatic village development, walls around the original village buildings including new buildings, a more strongly decorated village center, a correctly centered village map, a new test world run, and village-map contact can be unlocked by an actual visiting guest without the player personally visiting that origin village (26.11.2026).

## 1. Generation, Buildings, Mine and Identity

- G01: Orderly organic village/CTOV generation with larger spacing between houses, front gardens, trees, pastures and plots.
- G02: Every village, even with 2–3 residents, has a step-by-step infrastructure assignment from the start.
- G03: The central building-material warehouse accepts only building material.
- G04: The granary holds raw/cooked food, crops, meat and ingredients.
- G05: Separate tool/weapon/armor chamber, including repair goods.
- G06: School with classroom/benches, teaching of children and profession XP.
- G07: Kitchen/bakery with cooking pot, cutting board and ovens.
- G08: Later a train station/locomotive shed as a rail terminal.
- G09: Mix of small/medium/multi-story houses and functional buildings; palettes are based on the CTOV assets actually installed.
- G10: Market and town hall are central and leave expansion areas free; decorate the village center more richly.
- G11: Physically dismantle disruptive buildings, return the material, level/renaturalize the area and, where appropriate, rebuild suitably.
- G12: Terraced mine entrances, safe navigation without teleportation glitches or getting stuck.
- G13: An exhausted mine remains standing, is barricaded and is clearly labeled `[VERSCHLOSSEN - ERSCHÖPFT]` (in-game sign text: "[CLOSED - EXHAUSTED]"); the board/map call it Alte Mine (Old Mine).
- G14: Procedural biome-dependent village names; unknown neighbors become discoverable after a successful trade, a player/resident visit, or a real guest's arrival. The player need not personally visit a guest's origin village when that guest has already reached their village (owner clarification 26.11.2026).
- G15: Villages keep developing continuously and according to need without a player build assignment; a wall encloses the original buildings and new buildings.

## 2. Diplomacy, Roads and Railway

- D01: Persistent relations per neighboring village from -100 to +100.
- D02: Unknown, Neutral, Trade Partner from +30, Alliance from +60, Metropolis Union from +90.
- D03: From Trade Partner on, road builders of both villages build toward each other, clear 3 blocks of width and level the ground.
- D04: Fixed biome-appropriate cobblestone/gravel road with curbs, bridges over rivers/ravines and regular lamps.
- D05: Caravans use completed roads.
- D06: Railway only with Metropolis Union, civilization progress, minimum population of both places, smithy/mine resources and central warehouses.
- D07: Visible construction from real resources: rails, powered rails, redstone supply and roofed stations at both ends.
- D08: Automatic real chest minecarts transport harvest/wood/ingots; passenger trains carry commuters/market visitors.

## 3. Physical Building and Private Ownership

- B01: Professions procure raw materials, craft the required work blocks at a crafting table and place them themselves.
- B02: Visible preparation with scaffold/crafting table, foundation excavation/cobblestone, framework/supports, walls/windows/doors, roof, interior furnishing/decoration/scaffold removal.
- B03: Block placement shows tool/arm swing, sound and particles; dismantling shows continuous cracks 0–9 and tool swing.
- B04: House signs show name, function, residents, status.
- B05: Every resident has a private chest that community carriers do not take from.

## 4. Caravans

- K01: Own trader skin with travel clothing, backpack, hat.
- K02: Equipped chest donkeys instead of llamas.
- K03: Temporary edge camp with tent, fire, stakes and an armed guard.
- K04: Visibly carry real goods from the donkey chest to the village chest; buy surplus for actual emeralds.

## 5. Professions: Demand, Threshold, Work Cycle, Expansion, Maintenance

- J01: When food < residents × 5, the farmer independently expands fields including water/fences; harvest goes to the food store.
- J02: Cook/chef crafts kitchen blocks, cooks with Farmer's Delight/Chef's Delight ingredients and supplies taverns/households; meals give mood/efficiency bonuses.
- J03: Butcher feeds/breeds up to a pen cap >8, expands the stable and slaughters in a controlled way; meat goes to the food store.
- J04: Smith repairs at the anvil, smelts ores, replaces tools and produces rail material/minecarts.
- J05: Lumberjack visibly fells real trees, collects drops, replants, protects built-in wood and creates plantations after clear-cutting.
- J06: Stonemason/miner works in the active mine with supports/torches and produces gravel for roads/railway.
- J07: Carrier sorts building material/food/tools into the right depots and loads freight minecarts at the station.
- J08: Road/rail builder repairs and builds roads, rails, bridges and pillars.
- J09: Charcoal burner processes leftover wood into charcoal and supplies smithy/kitchen/lighting/steam locomotives.
- J10: Lamplighter checks light <1 and illuminates the village and overland routes.
- J11: Healer treats the injured with real brewing stand potions and the weakened with food.
- J12: Guard/lookout/adventurer staffs towers, patrols roads/railway, fights mobs and rings the alarm during raids.

## 6. Village Board (Dorftafel) and Administration

- U01: In-world block with rustic wooden frame, dynamic village name, uneven pinboard/parchments and 3D lanterns/iron fittings.
- U02: Header with village name/coordinates and manual renaming.
- U03: Interactive map, operable zoomed/panned and centered on the complete village.
- U04: Click buildings, assign, demolish and move them; show mine/school/warehouse/construction sites.
- U05: Mark zones on the map by drag and drop and save them.
- U06: Neighbors/contracts tab with relation, road/rail status and trade balance; the player approves major projects.
- U07: Sliders for profession quotas and child apprentice assignment.
- U08: Separate storage overviews/filters and target quantities; villagers deliver only up to the target, surplus stays private.

## 7. Labor Market and Daily Life

- A01: Abort a goal after 3 unsuccessful attempts, set a cooldown, choose alternative work.
- A02: In case of prolonged impossibility, temporarily switch to the most urgent profession.
- A03: Morning bell; during storms/thunderstorms end outdoor work, work indoors or seek shelter.
- A04: Activation also in Survival; residents do not stand idle in the center permanently.

## 8. Crafting and Procurement

- R01: Loaded vanilla/Fabric recipe management for all available craftable items including rails/minecarts/levers, correct ingredients/tags/components and leftover containers.
- R02: For permanently missing raw materials, a biome-appropriate substitute palette or caravan procurement with visible delivery.

## 9. Market, RPG and Presentation

- E01: Rented market stalls with visible goods; villagers buy food/tools/breeding stock from one another out of their private emerald holdings.
- E02: Hunger/mood directly affect work speed; end-of-work-day gathering in a real Village Taverns tavern with cooked meals.
- E03: Talk tab shows task, target coordinates, hunger/mood, private chest contents and missing building material.
- E04: Tools/freight visible in hands; own skins for traders/guards/train drivers/adventurers, compatible with Fresh Animations/EMF/ETF.
