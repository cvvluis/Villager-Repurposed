- Structured, loosened spacing: villages (including CTOV structures) generate in an orderly and organic way. Village houses deliberately stand somewhat further apart to leave room for front gardens, open areas, trees, small pastures and realistic plots instead of being packed tightly.
- Mandatory milestones for every village (specialized infrastructure):
- Every village – even a small hamlet with only 2 to 3 residents – has from the start the algorithmic construction order to work step by step toward specialized core buildings:
1. Central storage for building materials (village chest): takes exclusively building materials (wood, stone, brick, etc.).
2. Granary / food store: special pantry only for raw and cooked food, crops, meat and ingredients.
3. Tool & armor chamber (tool shed): standalone depot for new, used and to-be-repaired tools, weapons and armor pieces.
4. Village school: training site for children (classroom/benches) where the apprenticeship system is anchored and XP for professional qualifications is gathered.
5. Village kitchen / bakery: work area with cooking pots, cutting boards and ovens for the cook.
6. Train station / locomotive shed (late phase): terminal for rail connections at an advanced civilization level.
- Biome palettes & dynamic variety:
- Variability of building sizes and styles: every village consists of a mix of different building sizes and types (small one-room huts, medium-sized houses with extensions, multi-story workshops and functional buildings).
- Adaptation to in-game appearance: pictorial references such as image_3.png and image_4.png serve exclusively as conceptual examples of the range of biome styles. The mod must orient itself primarily to the assets actually generated in game, the block aesthetic and the palettes of the installed version of ChoiceTheorem's Overhauled Village (CTOV).
- Intelligent centering: market square and town hall deliberately seek central hubs and do not block primary expansion areas.
- Building correction, demolition & renaturation:
- Buildings that block paths or are misplaced are physically dismantled block by block by villagers.
- The demolition area is leveled (gravel/dirt) and cleanly renaturated if no direct new building follows.
- Reusable materials go into the building-material store; the building is rebuilt at a suitable location with sufficient free space.
- Mine lifecycle & "Verschlossen" (locked) mechanic:
- Mine entrances are terraced attractively; teleportation glitches and pathfinding hangs are eliminated completely.
- Exhausted mines: once an ore deposit is mined out, the shaft is never simply torn down. The entrance is barricaded with wooden planks/fences and gets a clearly visible sign reading [VERSCHLOSSEN - ERSCHÖPFT] (closed - exhausted).
- On the Dorftafel and the map, the entry is switched automatically from Mine to Alte Mine (old mine).
- Dynamic naming & trade diplomacy:
- Every village gets a procedural, biome-dependent name. Unknown neighboring villages initially appear in the mod system as undiscovered. Only after successful trade contact (through caravans or the player) is the village name revealed permanently and registered in the neighborhood overview.
2. City diplomacy, overland roads & railway network
- Diplomatic relations system (Town Relations):
- Every village keeps an internal relationship account with all known neighboring settlements (values from \(-100\) to \(+100\)).
- Status levels: Unbekannt (unknown) \(\rightarrow\) Neutral \(\rightarrow\) Handelspartner (trade partner, \(+30\)) \(\rightarrow\) Bündnispartner (ally, \(+60\)) \(\rightarrow\) Metropolen-Verbund (metropolis union, \(+90\)).
- Stage 1: permanent overland road building (from "Handelspartner"):
- Road builders of both villages work toward each other from their village borders:
- They clear a 3-block-wide corridor and level the terrain.
- They lay a fixed roadbed of biome paving stone, gravel and curbstones.
- They build simple wooden/stone bridges over rivers and ravines.
- Lamplighters place street lanterns/posts at regular intervals to light the road against mob spawns.
- Caravans use this road from then on.
- Stage 2: the railway age (from "Metropolen-Verbund" & civilization upgrade):
- Prerequisite: both villages have reached a minimum population and have sufficient smithing and mining resources (iron/coal) as well as central storage.
- Rail builders and stonemasons upgrade the roadbed with rails, powered rails and redstone power. Covered stations are built at the village ends.
- Automatic freight trains (chest minecarts) transport harvests, wood and ingots; passenger trains let commuters and market visitors travel between cities.
3. Physical construction & demolition flow (visible, observable & detailed)
- Autonomous crafting of work blocks:
- If a profession needs a station (e.g. furnace/cooking pot, anvil/blast furnace, crafting table, stonecutter), it fetches raw materials from the material chest, crafts the block autonomously at a crafting table and places it at its position.
- Observable house building in 5 phases:
- Construction is a visible event:
1. Preparation (scaffolding, crafting table on site)
2. Phase 1 (foundation excavation & cobblestone)
3. Phase 2 (frame & support beams)
4. Phase 3 (walls, windows & doors)
5. Phase 4 (roof truss & roofing)
6. Phase 5 (interior, decoration & scaffold removal).
- Visible placing & breaking:
- Placing happens with an arm swing, sound and particles.
- Breaking happens continuously with visible block cracks (stages 0–9) and tool swing.
- Village signs & private property:
- Every house gets a detailed sign (name, function, residents, status).
- Every resident owns a private chest (Personal Chest) that community carriers may not touch.
4. New caravan design, donkey logistics & rest stops
- Standalone trader design: specific skin for overland traders (travel clothing, backpack, hat), completely detached from the standard villager.
- Donkeys with chests (no more llamas): caravans travel with loaded donkeys (Chested Donkeys) on a lead.
- Caravan camp & rest stop: traders set up a temporary camp at the village edge (tarp, fire pit, hitching posts) together with an armed escort guard.
- Visible loading and unloading: scarce goods are physically carried from the donkey chests into the village chest; overproduction is bought for cash with emeralds.
5. Profession logic matrices & scaling for every job
Every profession acts in a closed control loop of need \(\rightarrow\) threshold \(\rightarrow\) work cycle \(\rightarrow\) self-expansion \(\rightarrow\) maintenance:
- Farmer: tends beds proportionally to the residents (\(food supply < residents \times 5\)). If the supply falls, he levels and expands new field clusters on his own, including water ditches and fences. Harvest goes directly into the food store.
- Cook / head chef (Farmer's Delight / Chef's Delight): crafts kitchen blocks independently, fetches ingredients from the food store, cooks full meals and supplies taverns and households for mood and efficiency bonuses.
- Butcher: feeds animals up to the pen cap (\(>8\) animals). When breeding reaches the limit, he builds visible stable extensions, slaughters in a controlled way and stores meat in the food store.
- Smith: repairs tools at the anvil, smelts ores, makes replacement tools for the depot and produces rails/minecarts for the later rail network.
- Woodcutter (Woodworker): fells trees in the surroundings in a targeted way with visible mining progress, collects drops, replants saplings and ignores built-in village wood. Lays out orderly plantations after clear-cutting.
- Stonemason / miner (Quarryman): mines blocks in the active mine, secures tunnels with support beams and torches. Breaks gravel for the railway and road substructure.
- Carrier (Carrier / Hauler): sorts with logistic precision: building material \(\rightarrow\) village chest; food \(\rightarrow\) food store; tools \(\rightarrow\) tool chamber. Loads transport minecarts at the station.
- Road & rail builder (Civil Engineer / Road Builder): repairs village paths, builds the overland roads to allied villages and, in the industrial age, lays railway roadbeds including bridges and supporting piers.
- Charcoal burner / kiln worker: turns leftover wood into charcoal in the furnace and supplies smithies, kitchens, lighting and steam locomotives with fuel.
- Lamplighter: checks light levels (\(<1\)) in the village area and along the overland roads and places torches/lanterns against monster spawns.
- Paramedic / healer: heals injured units with brewing stand potions and feeds exhausted villagers.
- Adventurer / guard / lookout: staffs watchtowers, patrols road connections, secures rail roadbeds against mobs and rings the alarm bell during raids.
6. Dorftafel: redesign & in-depth management
- Visual redesign of the block (in-world) & UI: see image_2.png. Rustic wooden strip with a dynamically rendered village name instead of the rigid banner, uneven notice-board look with parchment notes, postings, 3D lanterns/iron fittings on the posts and an interactive, zoomable and pannable map.

Separate overview and stock control for building materials, food and tools. Threshold control: villagers deliver only up to the configured target value; surpluses stay in the private chest.
7. Dynamic job market, anti-stuck & daily routines
- Anti-stuck-task logic: if a target block cannot be reached after 3 attempts, the villager aborts the task, puts it on cooldown and chooses an alternative action.
- Job timeout: if a profession cannot be practiced for a longer time, the villager automatically switches temporarily to the most urgently needed job.
- Daily routines & bell signal: in the morning the bell briefly rings to signal the start of work. During storms/thunderstorms workers stop outdoor work and switch to indoor crafts or seek shelter.
- Survival activation guarantee: fix for the bug that villagers stand idle in the village center in survival worlds.
8. Valid crafting & recipe subsystem
- Dynamic Fabric recipe check: direct access to the vanilla/Fabric recipe registry so villagers can produce all items (incl. rails, minecarts, levers) without errors whose ingredients are present. Fix for wrong or faulty NBT/tag conditions.
- Failover logic: if raw materials are missing permanently, the biome substitute palette applies or the donkey caravan delivers the goods and deposits them visibly.
9. Market economy, RPG stats & visuals
- Market stalls & B2B trade: villagers rent stalls, display goods physically and buy necessities (food, tools, breeding stock) from each other with their private emeralds.
- Hunger & mood: values directly influence work speed; after work is spent in the tavern (Village Taverns), where the cook's cooked dishes are eaten.
- Talk tab (extended dialogue): shows current task, target coordinates, hunger/mood, contents of the private chest and missing building materials.
- Visual presentation & animations: visible tools/cargo in the hands; own skins for traders, caravan guards, train drivers and adventurers; full animation support (Fresh Animations, EMF, ETF).
